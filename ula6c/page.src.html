Spectrum ULA Test Bench

A ZX Spectrum 48K whose ULA is the real logic, not a model of its behaviour: the Ferranti 6C001E-7 transcribed gate for gate from its logic diagram and simulated gate by gate, each with its delay. The Z80 runs on the ULA's own clock, so contention is whatever the gates do, and the picture comes from the ULA's colour lines. It is slow, a few per cent of a real Spectrum; it is for checking the ULA, not for playing.

Choose a Spectrum 48K ROM image (16K, 16,384 bytes). The ROM is not part of this page; it stays in your browser.

The black stripes at the left edge are on the ULA's colour lines at the start of each line in this simulation, where the emulator shows border. That difference is still being looked into (the README in the repository has where it stands).

Click the picture and type: Shift is CAPS SHIFT, Ctrl or Alt is SYMBOL SHIFT, Backspace is DELETE (CAPS SHIFT + 0) and the arrow keys are CAPS SHIFT + 5 to 8. The keys below are the Spectrum's rows; CAPS and SYMBOL latch for one key. At a few per cent of full speed, hold a key until the screen answers.

Each frame from the ULA's colour lines is compared, dot by dot, with what the bitmap and attributes in its lower RAM say the 256 × 192 picture should be. Flashing cells are left out (the ULA's flash counter starts where it starts), and a screen being drawn can differ for a frame.

Report

What is checked elsewhere. The testbench in the repository runs the emulator's Z80 against this ULA and compares cycle by cycle: contention (every hold agrees with Fuse's figures), the floating bus, port FE, ROM select, the CPU's reads of the lower RAM, the ULA's screen reads and the picture. Its README says where it stands.
