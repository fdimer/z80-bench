ZX81 ULA Test Bench

A ZX81 whose ULA is the real logic, not a model of its behaviour: the Ferranti 2C210 transcribed gate for gate from its logic diagram, or the single-clock design made from it for an FPGA. The Z80 and the picture get everything from the ULA's pins. Load the ROM, run programs, and report anything a real ZX81 would not do.

Choose a ZX81 ROM image (8K, 8192 bytes). The ROM is Sinclair's and is not part of this page; it stays in your browser.

Click the picture and type, or use the keys, which are marked as on a ZX81: the keyword above (what the key gives at the K cursor), the red symbol or word with SHIFT, and the function below (after SHIFT + NEW LINE). SHIFT latches for one key. Backspace is SHIFT+0 (RUBOUT). To load a program, choose a .p file: the page then types LOAD "" for you.

waiting

Every half second the dots on the ULA's TV pin are read back as characters and compared with the display file in RAM, using the ROM's own font. It only applies to ordinary character screens, and a screen that is being redrawn can differ for a moment.

Report

Logic analyser

The last 16,000 T-states of the ULA's pins and a few inside signals, one sample per period of its 13 MHz clock (four to a T-state). Recorded with the synchronous design; pause to read it. VCD is the format logic-analyser viewers such as GTKWave open.
